# Novelty / Innovation Claims

## 1. Hazard × Impact prioritization
Instead of ranking locations only by landslide probability, LandslideGuard estimates operational priority using both hazard and exposure.

## 2. Confidence-aware human-in-the-loop AI
A prediction with low confidence is not treated the same as a confident prediction. This enables verification queues.

## 3. Digital Twin for disaster planning
Operators can ask “what happens if rainfall increases by 50 mm?” before conditions become critical.

## 4. Citizen-to-model learning loop
Field reports and photographs are not dead-end complaints. They are designed as future labeled observations.

## 5. Multilingual, role-aware alert orchestration
The platform separates AI recommendation from authorized warning approval.

## 6. Offline-first architecture
Remote NER areas can continue collecting field observations locally and synchronize later.

## 7. Explainability
Top model factors are displayed alongside the prediction so an officer can challenge or validate the output.

## 8. Multi-source future fusion
The architecture can add InSAR deformation, NDVI, DEM derivatives, weather forecasts and IoT signals without rewriting the command center.
