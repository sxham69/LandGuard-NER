# 6-Minute SIH Judge Demo

## 00:00–00:45 — Command Center
“Here is the NER situation board. Every zone has hazard, confidence, exposure and operational priority.”

Point out that operational priority is not identical to hazard.

## 00:45–02:00 — Digital Twin
Select Shillong or Gangtok.

Increase:
- 24h rainfall
- 72h rainfall
- soil moisture
- slope

Say:
“The important part is not only the final color. We can see the AI's confidence and the dominant factors.”

## 02:00–03:00 — Impact
Explain:
“Suppose two slopes have similar hazard. One is isolated, the other is beside a strategic road. The response priority should not be identical.”

Show exposure and priority.

## 03:00–04:00 — GIS
Show the risk circles, strategic assets and field reports.

## 04:00–05:00 — Field intelligence
Submit a slope-crack image.
Explain the lightweight visual triage:
“It checks whether the image is usable evidence. It does not claim to diagnose a landslide.”

## 05:00–05:45 — Alert Center
Generate a CRITICAL Assamese/Bengali/Hindi alert.

Say:
“AI recommends; authorized officials approve. Every alert is auditable.”

## 05:45–06:00 — Close
“Our architecture is ready to accept IMD, IoT, Sentinel and GIS feeds. The hackathon prototype proves the decision loop end-to-end.”
